About NEO Chatbot

NEO Chatbot is an advanced AI-powered assistant designed specifically for the NEO Warehouse Management System. It combines multiple intelligent services including knowledge base search, SQL query assistance, and automated diagnostic support to provide comprehensive help and insights for NEO operations.

Powered by: FastAPI, LangChain, OpenAI/Groq/Anthropic, RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)
